[[Control]]

The Ziegler-Nichols rule is a heuristic [[Proportional-Integral-Derivative Controller (PID)]] tuning rule that attempts to produce good values for the three PID gain parameters given two measured feedback loop parameters derived from measurements:
- the period $T_{u}$ of the oscillation frequency at the stability limit
- The gain margin $K_{u}$ for loop stability
