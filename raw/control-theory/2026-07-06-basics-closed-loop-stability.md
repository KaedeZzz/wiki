[[Closed Loop Transfer Function]]

The closed loop poles are solutions of
$$
1+KG(z)=0
$$
re-writing rational function $G(z)$ as $b(z) / a(z)$, we have:
$$
1+KG(z)=\frac{{a(z)+Kb(z)}}{a(z)}
$$

- The **zeros** of $1+KG(z)$ are **closed loop poles**
- The **poles** of $1+KG(z)$ are **open loop poles**
