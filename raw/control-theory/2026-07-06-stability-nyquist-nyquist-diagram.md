[[Control]]

An Argand diagram of $G(e^{j\theta})$ as $\theta$ increases from $-\pi$ to $\pi$, or alternatively from $0$ to $2\pi$. Equivalently it is a mapping of trajectories from the unit circle $e^{j\theta}$ to the function $G(e^{j\theta})$.

![[Argand Diagram]]

**Nyquist diagram is symmetric about real axis.**
