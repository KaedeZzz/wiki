[[Control]]
$$
F(s)=\int_0^{\infty}f(t)e^{-st}dt
$$
where $s$ is a complex variable.

Used to investigate **transient** response of a system (and hence its stability).

*Note: Laplace transform assumes that input signal starts at time $t=0^{+}$.*
