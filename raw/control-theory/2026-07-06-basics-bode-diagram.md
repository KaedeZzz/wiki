[[Control]]

Filter amplification and phase shift can be characterised at each frequency. Therefore we plot Bode diagram: a plot of amplitude/phase shift vs. frequency (or radian) for steady state sinusoidal input.

Bode diagrams provide a good representation of the system behaviour since every signal at the input can be decomposed into sum of sinusoids.
