[[Nyquist Stability]], and [[Poles]]

Both denominator and numerator of $1+KG(z)$ has $n$ roots, some of which  are outside the unit circle and some within or on the unit circle. Therefore:
$$
N_{cp,i}+N_{cp,o}=N_{op,i}+N_{op,o}=l
$$
therefore,
$$
\mathcal C=N_{cp,i}-N_{op,i}=N_{op,o}-N_{cp,o}
$$
