[[Control]]

Let the transfer function of a [[Linear Time-Invariant (LTI)]] system be a [[Rational Function]] and has poles.

In these cases, the system will be unstable:
- _**Any**_ of the poles have a positive real part, or
- _**Repeated**_ poles on the imaginary axis

In this case, the system will be marginally stable:
- An _**Isolated**_ pole on the imaginary axis

In this case, the system will be asymptotically stable:
- _**All**_ poles have negative real parts.
