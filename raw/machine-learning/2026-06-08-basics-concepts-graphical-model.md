Factorisation of high-dimensional distribution can be encoded one-to-one as [[Graph]]s in which:
- **nodes** = random variables, and
- **edges** = direct dependence relations
- missing edges encode [[Conditional Independence]].

There are two main families of graphical models:
- [[Bayesian Network]]s
- Markov networks
