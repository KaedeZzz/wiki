The empirical [[Loss Function]], $\mathcal L_{emp}$, is computed from training data $\mathcal D=\{(\mathbf{x}_{i},y_{i})\}$:
$$
\mathcal L_{emp}= \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathcal L(f(x_{i},\theta),y_{i})
$$
