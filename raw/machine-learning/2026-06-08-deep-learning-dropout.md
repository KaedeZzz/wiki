Dropout can smooth overly specific fitted behaviour.

During training, randomly drop out (set to zero) a fraction of neurons (along with their connections) in a layer for each training batch.

The fraction is valued with probability $p$ known as the drop out rate. Typically use $\approx 0.2$.
