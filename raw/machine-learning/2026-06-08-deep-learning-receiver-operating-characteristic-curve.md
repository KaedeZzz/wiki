Curve of ***False positive rate vs. True positive rate***. 
Known as ROC curve.
The decision rule for biased [[Loss Function]] makes "cost ratio" an operating threshold; changing the threshold moves the *operation point* on ROC curve.
A single metric, area under ROC curve, is known as ***ROC-AUC score***.
