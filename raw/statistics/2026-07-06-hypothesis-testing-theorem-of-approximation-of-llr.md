[[Normalised Log-Likelihood Ratio (LLR)]]
Under $H_{1}$, where $X_{i}\sim$ i.i.d. $Q$:
$$
L L R\to D(Q||P)
$$
Under $H_{0}$, where $X_{i}\sim$ i.i.d. $P$:
$$
L L R\to-D(P||Q)
$$
