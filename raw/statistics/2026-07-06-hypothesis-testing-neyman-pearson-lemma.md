#ESSENTIAL 

If a thresholding [[Hypothesis Testing]] has probability of [[Type-I Error]] $\alpha$ and probability of [[Type-II Error]] $\beta$, then any other test that has type-I error probability $\leq \alpha$ necessarily has type-II error probability $\geq \beta$.
- No thresholding test can have lower probability for both types of errors.
