A sequence of vectors $\{ x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},\dots \}$ is a Cauchy sequence of vectors if for any positive $\epsilon>0$, there exists a number $N$ that
$$
\lVert x_{m}-x_{n} \rVert <\epsilon
$$
for all $m,n>N$.

This means that for a Cauchy sequence the terms get closer and closer in an unlimited manner.
