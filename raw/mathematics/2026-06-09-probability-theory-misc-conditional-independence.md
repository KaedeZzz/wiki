We say $X$ and $Y$ are conditionally independent (to condition $Z$) if
$$
P(X,Y|Z)=p(X|Z)P(Y|Z)
$$
Annotation:
$$
(X\perp Y|Z)
$$
Interpretation:
- once $Z$ is known, $X$ and $Y$ stop giving extra information about each other.
