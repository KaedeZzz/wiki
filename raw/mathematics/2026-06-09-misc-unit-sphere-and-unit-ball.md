Evaluated with [[Vector Norm]].

Unit ball:
$$
\{ \mathbf{x} \in \mathbf{V}:\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert \leq 1 \}
$$

Unit sphere:
$$
\{ \mathbf{x} \in \mathbf{V}:\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert = 1 \}
$$
