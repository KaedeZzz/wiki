A [[Probability Space]] is a [[Measure]] space $\{ X,\Sigma,\mu \}$ with $\mu(X)=1$.

To be exact:
- The set of objects is the sample space $\Omega$.
- The $\sigma$-algebra is usually denoted as $\mathcal{F}$ which will be defined in terms of all outcomes we want to consider, which is certain subsets of the sample space.
- The measure function $P$ is non-negative and between $0$ and $1$.
- $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},P)$ defines a probability space.
