#ESSENTIAL
[[Harmonic Solution of Wave Equation]]
Consider [[Dispersion]] relation: $\omega=kc$
In standing wave, $k=\frac{n\pi}{L}$
Therefore, the fundamental (lowest natural) frequency:
$$
\omega_{f}=\frac{\pi c}{L}
$$
*Note that: for comparison between continuous and discretised models, fundamental frequency of continuous model can be compared with frequency of first (discrete) mode for accuracy.*
