[[Autocorrelation]]
Autocovariance:
$$
\text{Cov}(X_{t},X_{t+\tau})=\mathbb{E}\{(X_{t}-\mathbb{E}(X_{t}))(X_{t+\tau}-\mathbb{E}(X_{t+\tau}))\}
$$

Then, autocorrelation:
$$
\frac{\text{Cov}(X_{t},X_{t+\tau})}{\sqrt{ \text{Var}[X_{t}]\text{Var}[X_{t+\tau}] }}
$$
