All eigenvalues of a [[Positive Semi-Definite]] matrix are non-negative. 
Proof:
$$
\begin{align}
\Sigma v&=\lambda v \\
v^{T}\Sigma v&=\lambda|v|^{2}\geq 0\implies \lambda\geq 0
\end{align}
$$

all eigenvalues of a [[Positive Definite]] matrix are strictly positive.
