Under what conditions is a [[Wide-Sense Stationary]] random process [[Ergodic]]? 

A **necessary and sufficient** condition for mean  is given by
$$
\lim_{ N \to \infty } \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}c_{X X}[k]=0
$$
where $c_{X X}[k]$ is the autocovariance function.
A simpler sufficient condition is that $c_{XX}[0]<\infty$ and
$$
\lim_{ N \to \infty }c_{XX}[N]=0
$$
