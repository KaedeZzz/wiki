The sequence of functions $f_{1}(x),f_{2}(x),\dots,f_{n}(x),\dots$ converges pointwise to $f$on domain $D$ if given $\epsilon>0$ there is a natural number $N=N(x,\epsilon)$ such that for $n>N$ it holds that $|f_{n}(x)-f(x)|<\epsilon$.
- **Note here $N$ is dependent on $x$ and $\epsilon$.**
- *The $N^{th}$ and later functions always have smaller difference.*
