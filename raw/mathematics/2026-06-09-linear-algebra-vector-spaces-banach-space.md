If a [[Normed Space]] is complete, it is called a Banach space.

All finite dimensional normed space are Banach spaces.

The space $V$ with elements $x=(x_{1},x_{2},\dots)$ that satisfies
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}|x_{i}|^{p}<\infty
$$
is a Banach space, known as $I^{p}$-space under the defined norm $I_{p}$.
