Greatest common divisor is the largest integer $d$ that divides both $n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$, denoted $\gcd(n_{1},n_{2})$.

Properties:
- $\gcd(0, n)=\lvert n \rvert$
- $\gcd(\pm n_{1},\pm n_{2})=\gcd(\lvert n_{1} \rvert,\lvert n_{2} \rvert)$
- $\gcd (n_{1}, n_{2})>0$ unless $n_{1}=n_{2}=0$
- $\gcd(n, 1)=1$
- $\gcd(n_{1}, n_{2})=1 \implies n_{1}, n_{2}$ are coprime
