$$
E(\theta)=-\log(P|\theta)-\log P(\theta)
$$
such that
$$
\frac{e^{-E(\theta)}}{Z}= \frac{P(D|\theta)P(\theta)}{Z}\propto P(\theta|D)
$$
which is Bayesian inference.
