[[Linear Algebra]]
Within a circuit:
$$
v_{flow}=Av_{node}, \quad A^{T}i_{flow}=0
$$
where $A$ is a matrix with $-1,0,1$ to express difference.
Considering that $v_{flow}$ is in the column space of $A$, and $i_{flow}$ is in the left nullspace of $A$, we therefore understand that:
$$
\sum_{k}v_{k}i_{k}=0
$$
