$$
\rho_{X,Y}=\text{corr}(X,Y)=\frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_{X}\sigma_{Y}}
$$
which satisfies $-1<\rho<1$.
If $\rho<0$, $X$ and $Y$ are _anticorrelated_
If $\rho=0$, $X$ and $Y$ are _uncorrelated_
If $\rho>0$, $X$ and $Y$ are _correlated_
