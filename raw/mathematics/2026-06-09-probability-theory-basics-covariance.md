$$
\text{Cov}(X,Y)=\mathbb{E}[(X-\mu_{X})(Y-\mu_{Y})]=\mathbb{E}[XY]-\mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y]
$$
If $\mathbb{E}[XY]=\mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y]$, then $X$ and $Y$ are said to be uncorrelated.
Independent indicates uncorrelated but the inverse is not necessarily true.
$$
\text{Var}[X+Y]=\text{Var}[X]+\text{Var}[Y]+2\text{Cov}[X,Y]
$$
