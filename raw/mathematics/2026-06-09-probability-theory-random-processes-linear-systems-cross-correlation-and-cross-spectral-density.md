[[Autocorrelation and Cross Correlation]]
[[Power Spectrum]]

The [[Discrete-time Fourier Transform (DTFT)]] of cross-correlation between input and output
$$
r_{XY}(n)=\mathbb{E}\{X_{k}Y_{k+n}\}
$$
is
$$
\Phi_{XY}(\theta)=H(\theta)\Phi_{XX}(\theta)
$$
