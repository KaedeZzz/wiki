For any integers $n$ ("dividiend") and $d\neq 0$ ("divisor"), there exists **unique** integers $q$ and $r$ such that
$$
n=qd+r
$$
and
$$
0\leq r<\lvert d \rvert 
$$
4 step proof:
1. There exists a pair
2. There exists a pair with smaller remainder if remainder is greater than divisor
3. uniqueness: assume there are two pairs, subtract them and show conflict
