Finds [[Greatest Common Divisor (gcd)]].

For $n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$, alternatively divide one of them with each other, and replace the dividiend $n_{1}$ with remainder $R_{n_{2}}(n_{1})$, until we get $0$ in remainder and one number is a multiple of another.
