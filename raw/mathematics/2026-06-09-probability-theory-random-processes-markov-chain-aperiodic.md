[[Markov Chain]]
The period of state $i$ is the **largest divisor** of *integer* $\{ n:(Q^{n})_{ii}>0 \}$, that is, *the fastest step it can take to return to the state itself*. 

If the period is $1$ we say the state is **aperiodic**.
