Let $\{ W_{t} \}$ be [[White Noise]] such that:
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbb{E}\{ W_{t} \}&=0 \\
R_{W}(k)&=\delta_{0,k}\omega_{W}^{2}
\end{align}
$$
The $MA(q)$ is the process where
$$
X_{t}=\sum_{i=1}^{q}b_{i}W_{t-i}+W_{t}
$$

$MA(q)$ is [[Wide-Sense Stationary]].
