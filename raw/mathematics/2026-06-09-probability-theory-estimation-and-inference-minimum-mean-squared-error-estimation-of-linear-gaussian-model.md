The [[Posterior Distribution]] of linear Gaussian model was obtained as 
$$
p(\theta|\mathbf{x})=\mathcal N(\mathbf{\theta}^{\text{MAP}},\sigma_{e}^{2}\mathbf{\Phi}^{-1})
$$
so
$$
\hat{\theta}^{\text{MMSE}}=\mathbb{E}[\theta|\mathbf{x}]=\theta^{\text{MAP}}
$$
