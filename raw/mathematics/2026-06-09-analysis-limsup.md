For a real sequence $(a_{n})$,
$$
\limsup_{n\to \infty}a_{n}=\lim_{ n \to \infty } (\sup_{k\geq n}a_{k})
$$
which tells the value that the sequence gets arbitrarily close to from below (infinitely often, even if the sequence does not converge)
