If $\mathbf{A}$ is a $n\times n$ matrix with all positive entries, then:
- there exists a real positive eigenvalue $\lambda_{\max}$ known as [[Perron Eigenvalue]].
- $\lambda_{\max}$ is strictly larger in magnitude than all other eigenvalues.
- $\lambda_{\max}$ has [[Algebraic Multiplicity]] of $1$, meaning that there is only $1$ corresponding eigenvector.
- the corresponding eigenvector has all positive components.
- the positive eigenvector is unique up to scaling.
