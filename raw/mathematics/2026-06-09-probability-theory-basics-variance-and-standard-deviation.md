Variance: [[Expectation]] of squared deviation
$$
\begin{aligned}
Var[X]&=\mathbb{E}[(X-\mathbb{E}[X])^{2}]\\
&=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\mathbb{E}[X]^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Standard deviation:
$$
\sigma_{X}=\sqrt{ Var[X] }
$$
