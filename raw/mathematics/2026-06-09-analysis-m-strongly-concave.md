A [[Probability Measure]] with density $\pi$ is $m$-strongly [[Concave]] if
$$
\log(\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y)\geq\lambda \log \pi(x)+(1-\lambda)\log \pi(y)+ \frac{m\lambda(1-\lambda)}{2}\lVert x-y \rVert ^{2}
$$
this is stronger than [[Log-concave]], satisfied by e.g. Gaussians.
