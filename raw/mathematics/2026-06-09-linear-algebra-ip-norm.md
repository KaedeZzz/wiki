A family of [[Vector Norm]]s:
$$
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert _{p}=\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n}\lvert x_{i} \rvert ^{p} \right)^{1/p}
$$

The $I_{1}$ norm is commonly known as the taxicab norm or *Manhattan distance*.
The $I_{2}$ norm is commonly known as the *Euclidean norm*.

$I_{\infty}$ norm:
$$
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert _{\infty}=\max_{i} \lvert x_{i} \rvert
$$
