$$
\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}=\mathbf{v}^{H}\mathbf{u}=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}u_{k}v_{k}^{*}
$$
thus
$$
\lVert \vec{v} \rVert ^{2}=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}v_{k}v_{k}^{*}=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\lvert v_{k} \rvert ^{2}
$$
Note that $\mathbf{v}^{H}\mathbf{u}=\overline{\mathbf{u}^{H}\mathbf{v}}$.
