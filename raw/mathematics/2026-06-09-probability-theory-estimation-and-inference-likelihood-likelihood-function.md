The observed data $\mathbf{x}$ can be considered random, and we can often obtain the [[Probability Density Function]] for $\mathbf{x}$ when the parameter $\mathbf{\theta}$ is known. This PDF is known as the *Likelihood*, which is defined as

$$
\mathcal L(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{\theta})=f_{\mathbf{X}|\mathbf{\Theta}}(\mathbf{x}|\mathbf{\theta})
$$
