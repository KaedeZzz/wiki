Let $\sigma_{i}=\pm 1$ be the indicator of the magnetic direction of each atom.

The hypothesis is that, assuming that only neighbouring atoms have interaction, the system energy depends on the number of neighbouring pairs of atoms in same or different direction. That is, the "energy":
$$
E(\sigma)=-\sum_{i,j}J_{ij}\sigma_{i}\sigma_{j}
$$
where 
$$
J_{ij}=
\begin{cases}
1 &  \text{if there is bond between atom }i\text{ and }j \\
0 & \text{if there is no bond}
\end{cases}
$$
