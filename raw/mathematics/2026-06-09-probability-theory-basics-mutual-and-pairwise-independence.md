Mutual Independence of [[Random Variable]]s:
$$
\prod_{i}p(A_{i})=p(\cap_{i}A_{i})\text{ for any combination of }i
$$
Pairwise Independence:
$$
p(A_{i})p(A_{j})=p(A_{i}\cap A_{j})\text{ for any }i,j
$$
