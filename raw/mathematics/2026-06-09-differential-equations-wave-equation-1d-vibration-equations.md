[[Wave Equation]]
###### Transverse Wave
1D vibration equation for *stretched string*:
$$
m \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial t^{2}}-P \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial x^{2}}=f(x,t)
$$
where $m$ is the mass, $P$ is the tension, and $f(x,t)$ is the external force applied to the string.

###### Horizontal Wave
1D vibration equation for *axial vibration of rod*:
$$
m \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial t^{2}}-EA \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial x^{2}}=f(x,t)
$$
where $E$ is the Young's modulus, and $A$ is the cross-sectional area.

###### Torsional Wave
1D vibration equation for *torsion of a shaft*:
$$
\rho J \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial t^{2}}-GJ \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial x^{2}}=T(x,t)
$$
where $\rho$ is the density, $J$ is the polar moment of area of the cross section, $G$ is the shear modulus of the material, and $T$ is the applied moment.

###### Acoustic Wave
1D vibration equation for *acoustic vibration in a duct:*
$$
\rho \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial t^{2}}-K \frac{\partial^{2}y}{\partial x^{2}}=f(x,t)
$$
where $K$ is the bulk modulus of the medium, and $f$ is the external pressure source.

Without external forcing, i.e. $f(x,t)=0$, the vibration equation takes the form of 1D wave equation.
