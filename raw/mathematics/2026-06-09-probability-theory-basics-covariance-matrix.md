$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\Sigma
\end{bmatrix}_{i,j}
=Cov(X_{i},X_{j})
$$
In matrix notation:
$$
\mathbf{\Sigma}=\mathbb{E}\{\mathbf{XX}^{T}\}-\mathbb{E}\{\mathbf{X}\}\mathbb{E}\{\mathbf{X}^{T}\}
$$
The matrix is [[Positive Semi-Definite]] and symmetric; it has a "square root" $\mathbf{ \Sigma}=\mathbf{SS}^{T}$.
