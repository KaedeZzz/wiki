if $\mathbf{u}\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{m},\mathbf{P})$ and $b\sim \mathcal{N}(0,\mathbf{C}_{b})$ are independent Gaussian random vectors, then
$$
\mathbf{Au}+\mathbf{b}\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{Am},\mathbf{APA}^{T}+\mathbf{C}_{b})
$$
