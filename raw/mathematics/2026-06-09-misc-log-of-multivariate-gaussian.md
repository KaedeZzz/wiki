log of a $d$-dimensional Gaussian:
$$
\log \mathcal{N}(x|\mu,\Sigma)=-\frac{1}{2}(x-\mu)^{T}\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)-\frac{1}{2}\log \lvert \Sigma \rvert -\frac{d}{2}\log(2\pi)
$$
