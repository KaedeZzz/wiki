A [[Probability Measure]] with density $\pi$ is log-[[Concave]] if
$$
\log \pi(\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y)\geq \lambda\log \pi(x)+(1-\lambda)\log \pi(y)
$$
more compactly,
$$
\pi(\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y)\geq \pi(x)^{\lambda}\pi(y)^{1-\lambda}
$$
