[[Random Variable]] and [[Discrete Distributions]]
$$
P_{X}(x_{i})=P(X=x_{i})
\implies \sum_{i}p_{X}(x_{i})=1
$$
The PMF is a complete description: for any set $A$,
$$
 P(X\in A)=\sum_{i=1}^\infty \mathbb{I}_{A}(x_{i})p_{X}(x_{i})
$$
