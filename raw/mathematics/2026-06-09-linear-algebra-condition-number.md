The condition number of an invertible matrix $\mathbf{A}$ is:
$$
\kappa(\mathbf{A})=\lVert \mathbf{A} \rVert \lVert \mathbf{A}^{-1} \rVert
$$
in the $2$-norm:
$$
\kappa_{2}(\mathbf{A})=\frac{{\sqrt{ \lambda_{\max}(\mathbf{A}^{H}\mathbf{A}) }}}{\sqrt{ \lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{A}^{H}\mathbf{A}) }}
$$
