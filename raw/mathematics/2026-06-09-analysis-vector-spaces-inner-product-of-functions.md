For functions defined by a variable on interval $x \in [a,b]$ of $\mathbb{R}$, the [[Inner Product]] between functions $f(\cdot)$ and $g(\cdot)$ is defined as
$$
\int_{a}^{b}f(x)g(x)dx
$$
