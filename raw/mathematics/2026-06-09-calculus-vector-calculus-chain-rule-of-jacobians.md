Given
$$
\begin{align}
h & :\mathbb{R}^{N}\to \mathbb{R}^{H} \\
g & :\mathbb{R}^{H}\to \mathbb{R}^{O} \\
f(x) & =g(h(x)) \\
f & :\mathbb{R}^{N}\to \mathbb{R}^{O}
\end{align}
$$
then
$$
J_{f}(x)=J_{g}(h(x))\cdot J_{h}(x)
$$
