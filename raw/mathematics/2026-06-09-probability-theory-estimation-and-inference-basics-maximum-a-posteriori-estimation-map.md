Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) estimator:
$$
\hat{\mathbf{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}}(x)=\arg\max_{\mathbf{\theta}}{ \{ f_{\mathbf{\Theta}|\mathbf{X}}(\mathbf{\theta}|\mathbf{x}) \} }
$$
