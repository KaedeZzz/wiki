Let $X_1,X_2,\dots$ be independent [[Random Variable]] with means $\mu_1,\mu_2,\dots$ and variances $\sigma_1^2,\sigma_2^2,\dots$, then the random variable

$$ S_n=X_1+X_2+\dots+X_n $$

tends to a [[Gaussian Distribution]] random variable $S$,

$$ S\sim\mathcal N(\mu_1+\mu_2+\dots+\mu_n,\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2+\dots+\sigma_n^2) $$

as $n\to\infty$, regardless of the actual individual distributions of $X_i$.
