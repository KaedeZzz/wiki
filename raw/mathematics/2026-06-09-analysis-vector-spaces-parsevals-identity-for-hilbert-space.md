For any function $f$ in a [[Hilbert Space]] $V$ with an infinite set of orthonormal vectors $\{ e_{i} \}$:
$$
\{ e_{i} \}\text{ is complete} \Longleftrightarrow \lVert f \rVert ^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}c_{i}^{2}\text{ with }c_{i}=\langle e_{i},f\rangle
$$
