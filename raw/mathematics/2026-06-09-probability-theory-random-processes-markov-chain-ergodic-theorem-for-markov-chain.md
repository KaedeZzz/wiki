When the [[Markov Chain]] is [[Irreducible]], then for any $\lambda$,
$$
\frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{k=0}^nr(X_{k})\to \sum_{i\in S}\pi_{i}r(i)
$$
indicates [[Ergodic]] Markov chain.
