Define [[Vector Norm]] that involves matrix $\mathbf{A}$ as:
$$
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert ^{2}_{\mathbf{A}}=\langle\mathbf{x},\mathbf{Ax}\rangle
$$
where $\langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle$ denotes inner product.
If the inner product is defined as the [[Inner Product of Complex Vectors]]:
$$
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert ^{2}_{\mathbf{A}}=\mathbf{x}^{H}\mathbf{Ax}
$$
