One-dimensional Gaussian measure: the [[Lebesgue Integral]]. Let $B \subset \mathbb{R}$, then
$$
g(B)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }}\int_{B}\exp\left( -\frac{1}{2}x^{2} \right)dx
$$
Define the infinite dimensional product measure on $\mathbb{R}^{\infty}$ as
$$
\mu=\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\mu_{n}\text{ where }\mu_{n}=g
$$
