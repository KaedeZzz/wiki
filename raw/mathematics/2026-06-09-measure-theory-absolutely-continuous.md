Consider two probability measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ on the same measurable space $\{ X,\Sigma \}$. If, for every set $A \in \Sigma$ where $\nu(A)=0$ it holds that $\mu(A)=0$, then $\mu$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $\nu$, denoted as $\mu\ll \nu$.
