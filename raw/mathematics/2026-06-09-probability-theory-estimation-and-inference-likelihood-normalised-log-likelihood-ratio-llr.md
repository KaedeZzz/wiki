[[Likelihood Function]]

Given the data $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$, the LLR is defined as:
$$
\frac{1}{n}\log \frac{Q(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})}{P(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})}
$$
