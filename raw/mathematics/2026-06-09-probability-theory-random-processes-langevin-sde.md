Langevin stochastic [[Differential Equations]]:

$$
dX_{t}=-\nabla U(X_{t})dt+\sqrt{ 2 }dB_{t}
$$
where $U:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to \mathbb{R}$ is called a potential and $(B_{t})_{t\geq 0}$ is the [[Brownian Motion]].
