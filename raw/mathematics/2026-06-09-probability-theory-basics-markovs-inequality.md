Bound upper tail probability.
For a non-negative [[Random Variable]] $X$ and any $a>0$,
$$
P(X\geq a)\leq \frac{\mathbb{E}\{X\}}{a}
$$
Note that this is useful only for $a>\mathbb{E}\{X\}$.
