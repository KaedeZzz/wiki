#ESSENTIAL 
[[Continuous Distributions]]
$$
f(x;\sigma)=\frac{x}{\sigma ^{2}}\exp\left( - \frac{x^{2}}{2\sigma ^{2}} \right)
$$
$$
F(x)=1-\exp\left( - \frac{x^{2}}{2\sigma ^{2}} \right)
$$
where $x \in [0,+\infty)$
