For unitary matrix $\mathbf{Q}$,
$$
\begin{align}
\lVert \mathbf{Qx} \rVert_{2} & =\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert_{2} \\ \\
\lVert \mathbf{QA} \rVert_{2} & =\lVert \mathbf{A} \rVert_{2}
\end{align}
$$
same applies to [[Frobenius Norm]].
