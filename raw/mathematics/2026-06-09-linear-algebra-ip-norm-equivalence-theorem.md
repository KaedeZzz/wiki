$\exists c_{1},c_{2}>0$ (typically dependent on the dimension of space) such that
$$
c_{1}\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert_{\alpha}\leq \lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert_{\beta}\leq c_{2}\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert_{\alpha}\quad \forall \mathbf{x}\in V
$$
