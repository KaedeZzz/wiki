The essence of using [[Monte Carlo]] approach to estimate quantities of a certain distribution is to assume that the distribution is estimated as
$$
\pi_{n}(x)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\delta_{x_{i}}(x)
$$
where $\delta_{x_{0}}(x)$ denotes the [[Dirac Delta Function]] located at $x_{0}$. Note how this functions satisfies as a probability distribution.
