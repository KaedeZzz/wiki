[[Random Variable]]
$$
\mathbb{E}[X]=\sum_{x \in \mathbb{X}}xP(x)\text{ or } \int_{x \in \mathbb{X}} xf(x)dx
$$
Expectation is a linear operator.

![[Law of the Unconscious Statistician]]

Conditional expectations: [[Conditional Probability]]
$$
\mathbb E[X|Y=y]=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xf_{X|Y}(x|y)dx
$$
