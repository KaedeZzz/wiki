A function is called convex if it always lies below any line segment joining two points on the function.

Mathematically, a function $f$ defined on an interval $(a,b)$ is called **convex** if for every $x_{1},x_{2} \in (a,b)$ and $\lambda \in [0,1]$, we have
$$
f(\lambda x_{1}+(1-\lambda) x_{2})\leq \lambda f(x_{1})+(1-\lambda)f(x_{2})
$$
