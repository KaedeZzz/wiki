If $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$ are independent random variables, then
$$
\mathbb{E}\left\{ \prod_{i=1}^{n}X_{i} \right\}=\prod_{i=1}^{n}\mathbb{E}\{X_{i}\}
$$
that is, *expectation of the product is the product of expectation.*
