Random variables $X,Y,Z$ are said to form a [[Markov Chain]] $X-Y-Z$ if their joint [[Probability Mass Function]] can be written as
$$
P_{XYZ}=P_{X}P_{Y|X}P_{Z|Y}
$$
i.e.
$$
P_{Z|Y}=P_{Z|XY}
$$
