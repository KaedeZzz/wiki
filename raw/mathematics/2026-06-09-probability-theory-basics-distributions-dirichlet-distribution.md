Generalisation of [[Beta Distribution]] to $m-1$ dimensional [[Simplex]].

The Dirichlet distribution is given by
$$
\text{Dir}(\pi|\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})=\frac{\Gamma\left( \sum_{i=1}^{m}\alpha_{i} \right)}{\prod_{i=1}^{m}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})}\prod_{i=1}^{m}\pi_{i}^{\alpha_{i}-1}
$$
