An orthonormal finite set of vectors $e_{1},e_{2},\dots,e_{n}$ is linearly independent iff
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}c_{i}e_{i}=0\implies c_{i}=0 \text{ for any }i
$$
Any orthonormal set of vectors serves as a *basis* for a a *finite dimensional* [[Inner Product Space]].
