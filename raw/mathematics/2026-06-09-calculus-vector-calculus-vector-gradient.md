[[Vector Calculus]]

$$
\frac{d\phi(\mathbf{\theta})}{d\mathbf{\theta}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{d\phi(\mathbf{\theta})}{d\theta_{0}} \\
\frac{d\phi(\mathbf{\theta})}{d\theta_{1}} \\
\vdots \\
\frac{d\phi(\mathbf{\theta})}{d\theta_{P-1}}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
