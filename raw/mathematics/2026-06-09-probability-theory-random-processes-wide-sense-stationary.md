A process $\{ X_{t} \}$ is wide-sense [[Stationary]] if:
- $\mathbb{E}\{X_{t}\}=\mu$ for all $t$
- $\text{Var}[X_{t}]$ is finite
- $\mathbb{E}\{X_{t}X_{t+k}\}=\mathbb{E}\{X_{0}X_{k}\}<\infty$ for all $t,k$
