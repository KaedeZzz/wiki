The inner product is a mapping from a pair of vectors $x$ and $y$ to a scalar satisfying the following:
- $\langle x,y\rangle=\langle y,x\rangle$ for vectors defined on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$
- $\langle \alpha x+\beta y,z\rangle=\alpha\langle x,z\rangle+\beta\langle y,z\rangle$ for $\alpha,\beta \in \mathbb{R}$
- $\langle x,x\rangle\geq 0$
- $\langle x,x\rangle=0$ iff $x=0$
