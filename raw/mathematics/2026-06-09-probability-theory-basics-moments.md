The $k$-th moment of a [[Random Variable]] $X$ is:
$$
\mu_{k}=\mathbb{E}\{X^{k}\}
$$
[[Expectation]] is the first moment; Variance (see [[Variance and Standard Deviation]]) is the second moment.
