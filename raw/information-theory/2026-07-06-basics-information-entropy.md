![[Information Content]]

Information entropy is the average of information content, in unit of bits:
$$
\mathbb{H}[X]=\mathbb{E}[I_{X}(x)]=-\sum_{x \in \mathbb{X}}P_{X}(x)\log_{2}P_{X}(x)
$$
*Information entropy describes "how much information do we expect to obtain from knowing the value of a random variable".*
