#ESSENTIAL 

*We can either use correlation (See [[Covariance and Correlation]]) or conditional entropy to describe relations between two random variables.*
*Correlation is prone to manipulations or "tempering" of distribution transformations, i.e. correlation can be uneasy to detect or easy to hide unless proper transformation is done to the distribution.*
*However, conditional entropy (see [[Joint and Conditional Entropy]]) easily reveal the relation, **not prone** to this kind of transformations.*
*Downside of conditional entropy is that we have to have knowledge of **full distributions** which could be difficult; correlation is easily computed from data.*
