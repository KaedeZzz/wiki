[[Channel Coding]]

![[Channel Capacity]]
$$
C=B\log_{2}\left(1+ \frac{S}{N}\right)
$$
where $B$ is the bandwidth of the channel, $S$ is the average received power over the bandwidth, and $N$ is the average noise power.
