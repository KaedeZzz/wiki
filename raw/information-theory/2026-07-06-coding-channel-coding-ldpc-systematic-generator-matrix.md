[[Linear Block Code]]

Systematic generator matrix is a generator matrix of the form
$$
G=[I_{k\times k}|P]
$$

The codeword corresponding to a length-$k$ data is:
$$
\underline{c}=\underline{x}G=[\underline{x}|\underline{x}P]
$$
where $\underline{x}P$ are parity bits.
