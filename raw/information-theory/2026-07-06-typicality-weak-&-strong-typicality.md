#ESSENTIAL 
Weak typicality:
$$
2^{-n(H(X)+\epsilon)}\leq P_{X^{n}}(x^{n})\leq 2^{-n(H(X)-\epsilon)}
$$
Strong typicality:
$$
P_{X}(0)-\epsilon\leq \frac{n_{0}}{n_{0}+n_{1}}\leq P_{X}(0)+\epsilon
$$
Example: A sequence generated by [[Bernoulli Distribution]] $Ber(0.5)$ cannot be compressed because all possible sequences are in the "weak typical" set. (Consider its $H=1$)
