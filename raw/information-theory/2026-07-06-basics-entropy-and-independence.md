#ESSENTIAL 

Joint entropy (see [[Joint and Conditional Entropy]]) and [[Independence]] of random variables are related as follows: for two independent random variables,
$$
H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y)\Leftrightarrow P_{XY}(x,y)=P_{X}(x)P_{Y}(y)\Leftrightarrow \text{Independence}
$$
*The nice thing about this is that, to prove that random variables are independent from **joint probability**, we need to go through every possible value of the distributions; however, via **entropy**, it can be proved with three single calculations.*
