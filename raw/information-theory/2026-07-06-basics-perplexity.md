[[Information Theory]]
Perplexity is a measure of uncertainty in the value of a sample from a discrete probability distribution.
$$
PP(p):=2^{H(p)}=2^{\sum_{x}p(x)\log_{2}p(x)}=\prod_{x}p(x)^{p(x)}
$$
