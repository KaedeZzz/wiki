[[Channel Coding]]

Channel capacity $C$ describes the theoretical tightest upper bound on the **information rate** of data that can be communicated at an arbitrarily low error rate.

USE PER CHANNEL!

In terms of mutual information: maximum mutual information between input and output of a channel, over all possible input distributions. The corresponding input distribution is called _optimal input distribution_.
$$
C_{Q}=\max_{\mathcal P_{X}}\ I(X;Y)
$$
