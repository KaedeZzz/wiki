Given a [[Block Code]] $\mathcal B$ with codewords $\{\underline{c}_{1},\dots,\underline{c}_{M}\}$, the optimal decoder is the one that minimises the probability of decoding error.

$$
\hat{\underline{c}}=\text{argmax}_{\underline{c}\in\{\underline{c}_{1},\dots,\underline{c}_{M}\}}Pr(\underline{y}|\underline{c})
$$
