[[Source Coding]]
The [[Expected Code Length]] of ***any*** uniquely decodable code satisfies
$$
\mathbb{E}\left\{ \frac{1}{n}\mathcal l(X^{n}) \right\}\geq H(X)
$$
*"You cannot do better than $H(X)$"*
Hence, ***entropy is the fundamental limit of lossless compression***
