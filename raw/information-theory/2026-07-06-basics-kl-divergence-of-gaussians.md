For two [[Gaussian Distribution]]s $\mathcal N(\mu, \sigma)$ and $\mathcal N(0, 1)$, [[KL Divergence]] has the following closed form:
$$
D_{KL}=- \frac{1}{2}(1+\log \sigma ^{2}-\mu ^{2}-\sigma ^{2})
$$
