A Discrete Memoryless Source (DMS) is a sequence of i.i.d. random variables $V_{1},V_{2},\dots$, all taking values in alphabet $\mathcal{V}$ with distribution
$$
P_{V^{n}}(v_{1},\dots,v_{n})=\prod_{i=1}^{n}P_{V}(v_{i})
$$
where $P_{V}$ is the common distribution.
