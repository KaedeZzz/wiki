Let $X$ be a random variable from  [[Bernoulli Distribution]]  that takes value 1 with probability $p$ and 0 with probability $1-p$. Its [[Information Entropy]] is
$$
\mathcal H_{2}(p)=p\log \frac{1}{p} + (1-p)\log \frac{1}{1-p}
$$
