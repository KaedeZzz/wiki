[[z-Transform]]

Suppose that all the poles of $(z-1)Y(z)$ lie strictly inside the unit circle. Then
$$
\lim_{ k \to \infty } y_{k}=\lim_{ z \to 1 } (z-1)Y(z)
$$
**must check poles** before applying!
