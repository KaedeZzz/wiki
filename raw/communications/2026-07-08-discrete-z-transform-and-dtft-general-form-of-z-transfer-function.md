$$
G(z)=\frac{b_{0}+b_{1}z^{-1}+\dots+b_{m}z^{-m}}{(1-p_{1}z^{-1})^{n_{1}}+\dots+(1-p_{r}z^{-2})^{n_{r}}}
$$
where $p_{i}$ is the $i$-th pole of the transfer function, and $\sum_{i}n_{i}=n>m$.

*This is the most general form (allow repeated poles) of z-transfer function with poles explicitly in the expression; always start from this when answering generic proof questions!*
