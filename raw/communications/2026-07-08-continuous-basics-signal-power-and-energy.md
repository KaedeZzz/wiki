[[Fourier Analysis]]
Total energy of a signal $f(t)$:
$$
E_{f}=\int^{+\infty}_{-\infty}|f(t)|^{2}dt
$$
Power:
$$
E_{f}=\lim_{ T \to \infty } \frac{1}{T}\int^{+\infty}_{-\infty}|f(t)|^{2}dt
$$
For any signal, only one of either is properly defined.
