An **i.i.d.** discrete-time random process $\{ \dots,X_{-1},X_{0},X_{1},X_{2},\dots \}$ with
$$
f_{X_{k_{1}},X_{k_{2}},\dots,X_{k_{n}}}(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})=f_{X}(x_{1})\cdot f_{X}(x_{2})\cdots f_{X}(x_{n})
$$

White noise is stationary and ergodic.

Note that the term [[White Noise]] is misleading: it is not necessarily "something disturbing that you want to get rid of". 
- Data, when properly compressed, is uniform and identically distributed: data is white noise!
- And noise is not necessarily white: most real world channels exhibit "echo" which results in non-independent noise.
