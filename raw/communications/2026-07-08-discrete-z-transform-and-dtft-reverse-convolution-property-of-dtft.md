[[Discrete-time Fourier Transform (DTFT)]]

For the DTFT,
$$
w_{n}=u_{n}v_{n}\implies W(\theta)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}U(\phi)V(\theta-\phi)d\phi
$$
*Multiplication of two time domains sequences result in periodic convolution of their spectra.*
