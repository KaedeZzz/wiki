For [[Fourier Series]]:
$$
\frac{1}{T}\int_{0}^{T}\lvert g(t) \rvert ^{2}dt=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\lvert c_{n} \rvert ^{2}
$$
For [[Fourier Transform]]:
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert f(t) \rvert ^{2}dt=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\lvert F(\omega) \rvert ^{2}d\omega
$$
