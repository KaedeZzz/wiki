Relationship between [[Fourier Transform]] and [[Laplace Transform]]:
$$
F(\omega)=\bar{f}(s=j\omega)
$$
which is the frequency response of the system.
Therefore, Laplace is better suited to problems with boundary conditions at $t=0$, and Fourier is better suited to steady state analysis.

When compared, 
- Fourier transform is more used to investigate and design **stationary** response; 
- Laplace transform is more used to investigate and design **transient** response.
