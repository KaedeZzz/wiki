[[Parseval's Theorem]] for [[Discrete Fourier Transform]]: if $\vec{X}=\mathbf{F}\vec{x},\vec{Y}=\mathbf{F}\vec{y},$
$$
\vec{X}\cdot \vec{Y}=N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}x_{k}y_{k}^{*}
$$
