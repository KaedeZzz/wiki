The $(N\times N)$ Fourier matrix stacks the $N$ Fourier vectors:
$$
W=\begin{bmatrix}
\vec{\omega_{0}}|\dots|\vec{\omega_{N-1}}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
which is defined from the $N$ complex roots of unity.
