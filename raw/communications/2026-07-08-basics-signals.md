[[Signal Processing]]
Continuous signal: signal that is continuous in **both** amplitude and time. Usually mathematically defined as a function $f(t)$ where amplitude is a function of time.

Discrete-time signal: signal that is continuous in amplitude **but** discrete in time. Usually mathematically defined as a sequence $\{ u_{k} \}$ where $k$ denotes the $k$-th timestep.

Discrete (digital) signal: signal that is discrete in **both** amplitude and time. Usually defined as a sequence $\{ u_{k} \}$ where amplitude is also discretized.
