[[z-Transform]]

$$\mathcal Z\{ 1,q,q^{2},q^{3},\dots \}= \frac{1}{1-qz^{-1}}=\frac{z}{z-q}$$

Region of convergence: outside circle of radius $\lvert q \rvert$.
