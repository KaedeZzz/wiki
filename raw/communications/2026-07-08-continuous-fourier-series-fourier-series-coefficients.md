[[Fourier Series]]

Relationship between real and complex coefficients:
$$
2c_{n}=a_{n}-jb_{n},\quad 2c_{n}^{*}=a_{n}+jb_{n}
$$
conversely solving for $a_{n}$ and $b_{n}$:
$$
a_{n}=c^{*}_{n}+c_{n},\quad jb_{n}=c^{*}_{n}-c_{n}
$$
we can also see that:
$$
c_{-n}=c^{*}_{n},\quad 2\lvert c_{n} \rvert =\sqrt{ a_{n}^{2}+b_{n}^{2} }
$$
