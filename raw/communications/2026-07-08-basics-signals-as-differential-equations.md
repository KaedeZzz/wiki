[[Differential Equations]]

Solution of an ordinary differential equation consists of two components: 
- complementary function (*does not decay*), and 
- particular integral (*decays*). 

In the field of [[Signal]] processing:
- **Complementary Function $\implies$ Transient Response**
- **Particular Integral $\implies$ Steady-state Response**
