![[Autocorrelation]]

Cross correlation: $\text{Cov}(X_{t},Y_{t+\tau}) / \sqrt{ \text{Var}[X_{t}]\text{Var}[Y_{t+\tau}] }$ 
If stationary: denoted as $C_{xy}(\tau)$

Cross spectral density function: [[Fourier Transform]] of $C_{xy}(t)$
