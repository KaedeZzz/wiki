[[Discrete-time Signals]]

FIR (*finite impulse responses*) and IIR (*infinite impulse response*) are impulse responses that differ in termination of sequence.

FIR:
$$
\{ g_{k} \}_{k\geq 0}=\{ g_{0},g_{1},\dots,g_{l},0,0,\dots \}
$$
IIR:
$$
\{ g_{k} \}_{k\geq 0}=\{ g_{0},g_{1},g_{2},\dots \}
$$
which does not terminate.
