[[Signal Processing]]

A formal power series uses a formal variable, where we never assign a value to the variable.

Do we use formal power series or "real" power series?
- We use *formal* power series to *map convolutions to multiplications* when there is no need to assign value to dummy variable or worry about convergence. (Generating functions, D transform)
- We use *"real"* power series where we assign a value to dummy variable to see *stability* and determine steady state *response* of signals. (z-transform and DTFT)
