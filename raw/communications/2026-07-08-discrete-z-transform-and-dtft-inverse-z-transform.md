Since power series is ***unique***, any valid method to find the power series of the [[z-Transform]] gives valid inverse of z-Transform.

Several methods:
1. **Partial fractions**
2. Polynomial (long) division
3. Express power series as a Taylor expansion
4. Inverse integral (based on residue theorem of complex analysis)
5. Inverse DTFT
