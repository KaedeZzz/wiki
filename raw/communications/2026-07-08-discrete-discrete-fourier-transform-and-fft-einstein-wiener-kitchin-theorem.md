$$
\lim_{ N \to \infty } \mathbb{E}\left[ \frac{1}{2N+1}\lvert X^{N}(e^{j\Omega}) \rvert^{2}  \right]=S_{x}(e^{j\Omega})
$$
The power spectrum is the *expected value of the (time-normalised) DTFT-squared* of the signal values.
