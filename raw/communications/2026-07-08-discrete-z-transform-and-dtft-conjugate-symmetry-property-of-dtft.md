If a discrete-time signal is ***real***, 
$$
S(\bar{z})=\overline{S(z)}
$$

For [[Discrete-time Fourier Transform (DTFT)]] and [[z-Transform]] on the unit circle, this results in
$$
\begin{cases}
|S(e^{-j\theta})|=|S(e^{j\theta})| \\
\angle S(e^{-j\theta})=-\angle S(e^{j\theta})
\end{cases}
$$
