[[Digital Filtering]]

![[Pasted image 20250102150300.png]]

A typical filter specification must specify *maximum permissible deviations* from the ideal:
1. band edge frequencies or corner frequencies
2. maximum passband ripple: *maximum magnitude deviation from unit gain*
3. minimum stopband attenuation: *minimum magnitude reduction from unit gain at stopband*
