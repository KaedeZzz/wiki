[[Signals]]
$$
s=(\dots,s_{-2},s_{-1},s_{0},s_{1},s_{2},\dots)
$$
Discrete-time signals is essentially a sequence of numbers; they can take values of real numbers, *complex numbers, and elements of finite fields* (for example $\{ 0,1 \}$).
