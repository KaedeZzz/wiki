![[Discrete Fourier Transform]]

The discrete [[Fourier Transform]] has the matrix representation
$$
X=W^{*}x\quad \text{and}\quad x=\frac{1}{N}WX
$$
where $x$ is a vector representation of ***discrete-time periodic*** signal.
