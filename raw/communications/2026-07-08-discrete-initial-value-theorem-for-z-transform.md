For [[z-Transform]]:
$$
\begin{align}
\lim_{ z \to \infty } U(z) &= \lim_{ z \to \infty } \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}u_{k}z^{-k} \\
&= \lim_{ z \to \infty } \left( u_{0}+ \frac{u_{1}}{z} + \frac{u_{2}}{z^{2}}+\dots \right) \\
&=u_{0}
\end{align}
$$
