Let $e^{j 2\pi/N}=\omega$, the N Fourier vector is:
$$
\vec{ \omega}_{k}=\begin{bmatrix}
\omega^{k 0} \\
\vdots \\
\omega^{k(N-1)}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
which are vector representations of the N-periodic discrete-time exponential signals.
