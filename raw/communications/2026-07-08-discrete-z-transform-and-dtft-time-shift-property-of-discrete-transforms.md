![[Time shift property of DTFT]]

Unfortunately the shifting is not so straightforward for [[z-Transform]] because z-transform is one-sided.
1. Shifting the signal by a positive $d$ ('delaying') makes signal values that are formerly ignored (because they were at negative times) now appear in z-transform.
2. Shifting the signal by a negative $d$ ('advancing') makes signal values that were formerly in z-transform must now be ignored.
