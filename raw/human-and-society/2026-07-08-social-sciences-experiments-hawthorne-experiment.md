###### Purpose
Effects of work conditions on performance.

###### Experimental Design
Sample: 3 female workers from Hawthorne Plants of Western Electric Co.
Initial conditions:
1. Adopted [[Scientific Management]]
2. 48 hours pw of work
3. No break

###### Results

![[Pasted image 20241014195052.png]]

The difference in first and last experiments, despite **the same working condition**, is explained by that ***workers respond favourably to the special attention they received***. 
Besides design of work, ***psychological factors matter.***
