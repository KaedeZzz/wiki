Based on Carl Jung's personality type theory and Jung Typology Test.
###### Dimensions
- Extroverted / Introverted
- Sensing / Intuitive: 
	order and detail vs. big pictures and gut feelings
- Thinking / Feeling:
	reason and logic vs. emotions
- Judging / Perceiving:
	control-oriented vs. flexible and spontaneous

###### Several Problems of MBTI
1. Invalid *categorisation* of personality traits
2. Not based on scientific findings
3. **Not predicting organisational outcomes**
