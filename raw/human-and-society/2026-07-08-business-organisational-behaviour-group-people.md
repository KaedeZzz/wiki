Two or more people interacting **inter**dependently to achieve a common goal.

Two types of work groups:
- Formal: a group established by **organisations** to facilitate the achievement of organisational goals.
- Informal: a group that emerges naturally in response to the common interests of organisational members.

Group assumptions:
- Synergy: group performance better than assembly of individual performance
- Effectiveness: group is better in achieving organisational goals
- Efficiency: groups are more efficient

**Group decision making**
Strengths:
- More complete information and knowledge
- Increased diversity of views
- Increased acceptance of better solutions
- Diffusion of responsibility

Weaknesses:
- slow
- conformity pressure
- social loafing
