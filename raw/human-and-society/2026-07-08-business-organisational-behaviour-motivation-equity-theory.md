Individuals **compare** their **outcome-input ratio** with outcome-input ratio of a referent.

Motivation is highest when organisational members perceive that they are treated equitably, that their outcome-input ratios are in balance.

Limitations:
- people interpret inputs and outcomes differently. Cognitive biases!
- emphasises extrinsic rewards.
