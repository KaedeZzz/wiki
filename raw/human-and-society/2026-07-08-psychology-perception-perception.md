[[Psychology]]

Perception is a process by which individuals interpret and organise their **sensory impressions** to give **meaning** to their environment.

Categorisation: 

**Relationship** based vs. Rule **based**
- Perception by **family resemblance** vs. Perception by **rule**

**Holistic** vs. **Analytic** Perception
- Prefer to see the entire field vs. observe focal object and analyse it

Why is it important? 
- Small differences cause huge differences in organisational decision making.
- Perception matters for business

Another important implication: people perceive the same objective stimuli differently.
- Perceptual biases may exist
