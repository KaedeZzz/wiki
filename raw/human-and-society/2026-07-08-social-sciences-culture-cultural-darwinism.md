*The fittest cultures survive over time.*

Central assumptions:
- organisational effectiveness is determined by how organisation adapts to the external environments.
- cultures adapt to external environments.

Basic tenet:
- External changes determine culture changes, and the changed cultures help the organisation best adapt to the external environments.
