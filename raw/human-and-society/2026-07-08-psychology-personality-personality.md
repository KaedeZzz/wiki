[[Business and Management]]
Personality refers to a person's *general* style of interacting with the world, especially other people.
- Personality is a **trait** that is less likely to change.
Research shows that people have a naive belief that *they evaluate their and others' personalities easily*.

Indicators: [[Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)]] and [[The Big Five]].
