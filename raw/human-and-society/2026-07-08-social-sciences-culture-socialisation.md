A process that adapts employees to the [[Organisational Culture]]s.

Antecedents: 
- socialisation is influenced by statements and behaviours exhibited by people and management.

Outcomes:
- previously dissimilar employees adjust to *conform* to organisational [[Norm]]s and become increasingly *similar* to other members.
