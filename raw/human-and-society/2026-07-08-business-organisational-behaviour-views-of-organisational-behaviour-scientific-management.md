Studying tasks carefully and systematically at the **micro level** to **speed up** work. Known as Taylorism. See: [[Frederick Taylor]]

***Key: 'system' is important, human = machine, standardised workflows & incentives***

###### Method

![[Time and Motion Studies]]

Henry Ford and Ford motor company put scientific management to the highest level with their assembly lines.

###### Outcomes

Pros:
1. Boost in productivity.
2. More profit for companies.
3. More consistent products of higher quality.

Other outcomes:
1. Companies often fail to pay more.
2. "Managers think, employers do" became normal.
3. Workers separated from the greater meaning of work.
4. Deskilled , expendable employees.
5. Harsh atmosphere in working.
6. Employee dehumanised and mental anguish.
