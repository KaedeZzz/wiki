Two kinds of motivations: intrinsic motivation vs. extrinsic motivation.

Intrinsic motivation has stronger effects on performance.

How to improve intrinsic motivation? -- people prefer to feel they have control over actions (i.e. **self-determination**). Once they feel their action is more self-determined, they will have higher intrinsic motivation.

Monetary incentives make people feel that main cause of their action is external factor, thus have lower sense of self-determination.
