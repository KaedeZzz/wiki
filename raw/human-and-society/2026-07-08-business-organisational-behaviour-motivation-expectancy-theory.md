You need all to be motivated:
1. Effort to performance (**expectancy**)
2. Performance to reward (**instrumentality**)
3. Rewards to personal goals (**valence**)

individual effort $\to$ individual performance $\to$ organisational rewards $\to$ personal goals and needs

Predictor of expectancy:
- ability
- well-defined performance goals
- trustworthy performance evaluation system

Predictor of instrumentality:
- good understanding of performance-reward relationship
- trustworthy relationship
- fair relationship -- people are hyper-sensitive to violations of fairness

Predictor of valence:
- refer to content-based theories

![[Equity Theory]]

Limitations:
- performance is not always measurable.
- organisations should create "personalised" rewards to increase valence.
