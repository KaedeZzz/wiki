[[Psychology]]

$$
\text{Performance}=f(\text{Motivation}\times\text{Ability})
$$
Ability is the mental or physical capacity to do something.

Motivation is the **extent** to which persistent effort is directed toward the goal.

$$
\text{Motivation}=f(\text{Effort},\text{Persistence},\text{Goal})
$$
Effort $=$ how hard a person tries
Persistence $=$ how long a person tries
Goal $=$ objective that a person tries to reach

Frameworks for understanding motivation:
- Content-based theories, offer ways to profile individual's needs
- Process-based theories, about cognitive processes influencing motivation
- Contemporary theories

###### Content-based Theories
