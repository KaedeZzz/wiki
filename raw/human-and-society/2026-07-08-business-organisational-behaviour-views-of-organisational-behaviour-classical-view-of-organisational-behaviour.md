Classical view focuses on non-human factors, that is, ***system***; following 'optimal system' leads to 'optimal performance'.

Assumptions:
1. Employees are **not self-motivated**
2. Employees are unable to devise effective **strategies**
3. Employees' mind is a black box that cannot be unveiled by managers
