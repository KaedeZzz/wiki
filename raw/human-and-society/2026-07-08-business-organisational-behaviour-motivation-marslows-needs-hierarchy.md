The following five needs are arranged hierarchically:
- Physiological needs
- Safety needs
- Belongingness and love needs
- Esteem needs
- Self-actualisation

After front needs satisfied, consequent needs become dominant.

Strengths:
- intuitive
- identifying universal needs

Limitations:
- cultural and personal differences in needs hierarchy
