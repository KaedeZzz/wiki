Developed using scientific methods:
1. Generating hundreds of personality items
2. Participants rated their personality using the items
3. Ran confirmatory factor analysis to generate (eventually 5) dimensions.
4. Validated in many different cultures.

5 Dimensions:
***Extraversion***
***Agreeableness*** (the degree to which a person is willing to defer to others)
***Conscientiousness*** (a measure of personal consistency and reliability, *most important for companies*)
***Emotional Stability*** (ability to withstand stress)
***Openness*** to experience

- Does not force personality into categories
- Based on scientific findings
- Highly related to organisational outcomes.
