Consider dynamical systems of the form:
$$
\frac{d}{dt}\mathbf{x}(t)=\vec{f}(\mathbf{x}(t),\mathbf{u}(t),t)
$$
where $\mathbf{x}$ is the state of the system and $\vec{f}$ is a vector field that possibly depends on the state $\mathbf{x}$, input $\mathbf{u}$, and time $t$.
