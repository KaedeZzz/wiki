#ESSENTIAL 
[[Vibration Modes]]

Continuous vibration can be approximated by discrete model of distributed mass; $n$ discrete modes may correspond to *the first $n$ "fundamental" frequencies*. (See EP1 Q3)

Discrete modes of lower frequencies would have more *accurate* approximations than higher frequencies.
