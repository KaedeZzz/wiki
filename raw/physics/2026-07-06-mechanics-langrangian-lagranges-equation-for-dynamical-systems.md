For a dynamical system,
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt} (\frac{{\partial T}}{\partial \dot{q_{j}}}-\frac{{\partial V}}{\partial \dot{q_{j}}})-(\frac{{\partial T}}{\partial q_{j}}-\frac{{\partial V}}{\partial q_{j}})&=Q_{j} \\
\frac{d}{dt} \frac{{\partial T}}{\partial \dot{q_{j}}}-\frac{{\partial T}}{\partial q_{j}}+\frac{{\partial V}}{\partial q_{j}}&=Q_{j}
\end{align}
$$
where $q_{j}$ are generalised coordinates, and $Q_{j}$ is the external force.
