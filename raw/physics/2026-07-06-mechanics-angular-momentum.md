Define the total angular momentum (moment of momentum) of a set of particles (or a rigid body) about any chosen point $P$ be
$$
\mathbf{h}_{P}=\sum_{i}(\mathbf{r}_{i}-\mathbf{r}_{P})\times m_{i} \dot{\mathbf{r}}_{i}
$$
