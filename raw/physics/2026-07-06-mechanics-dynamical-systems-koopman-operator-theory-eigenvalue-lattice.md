In discrete-time:
$$
\mathcal K_{t}(\psi_{1}(x)\psi_{2}(x))=\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\psi_{1}(x)\psi_{2}(x)
$$
This indicates that there may be a finite set of *generator* eigenfunction elements that may be used to construct all other eigenfunctions.
