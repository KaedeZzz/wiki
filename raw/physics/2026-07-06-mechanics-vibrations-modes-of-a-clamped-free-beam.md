$$
\begin{align}
U(0) & =0 \implies D_{1}+D_{3}=0\\
U'(0) & =0 \implies D_{2}+D_{4}=0\\
U''(L) & =0 \implies D_{1}(-\cos kL-\cosh kL)+D_{2}(-\sin kL-\sinh kL)=0 \\
U'''(L) & =0\implies D_{1}(\sin kL-\sinh kL)+D_{2}(-\cos kL-\cosh kL)=0
\end{align}
$$
Put in to matrix form and set determinant to zero to give
$$
\begin{align}
(\cos kL+\cosh kL)^{2} & =-(\sin kL-\sinh kL)(\sin kL+\sinh kL) \\
\cos kL\cosh kL & =-1
\end{align}
$$

Approximate non-zero solutions are
$$
kL\approx\left( n-\frac{1}{2} \right)\pi
$$
