$$
M \ddot{\mathbf{r}}_{G}=\dot{\mathbf{p}}=\mathbf{F}^{(e)}
$$
where: 
- $M$ is the mass of the rigid body
- $\ddot{\mathbf{r}}_{G}$ is the acceleration of the [[Centre of the Mass 1]]
- $\mathbf{p}$ is the linear momentum of the body 
- $\mathbf{F}^{(e)}$ is the total external force
