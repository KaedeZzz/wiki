If we know the [[Transfer Function of Vibration]] $g(t)$ from point $a$ to $x$, we can find the response to **any** time-varying force applied at the same point by doing:
- $y(t)=g(t)*f(t)$, or
- compute [[Fourier Transform]] of input and transfer function, and then multiply in frequency domain $Y(\omega)=G(\omega)F(\omega)$
