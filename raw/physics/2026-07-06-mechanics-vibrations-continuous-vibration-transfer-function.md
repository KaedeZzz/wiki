By regarding a continuous system as a limit of discrete systems:
$$
G(x,y,\omega)=\frac{U(y)}{F(x)}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{u_{n}(x)u_{n}(y)}{\omega_{n}^{2}-\omega ^{2}}
$$

Note that the results rely on [[Orthogonality of Eigenmodes]], and [[Mass-normalisation of Eigenmodes]] is required before the formula is used.
