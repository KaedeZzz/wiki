For a system with many resonances, define the modal overlap factor as
$$
\beta=\frac{\Delta\omega}{\omega_{n+1}-\omega_{n}}
$$
where $\Delta\omega$ is the half power bandwidth.

Low modal overlap $\beta\ll 1$: bandwidth is small w.r.t. spacing of peaks.
- modal peaks are well separated.
- response near resonance is well approximated by a single DOF
- response between two resonances is well approximated by summing modes on the either side.

High modal overlap:
- at any given frequency, many modes contribute to response.
- statistical methods apply widely (especially for high frequencies).
