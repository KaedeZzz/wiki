The bode diagram has the characteristics that:
- Asymptotic point at each $\omega_{n}$
- Asymptotic direction dependent on sign of ${u_{k}^{(n)}u_{j}^{(n)}}$
- Existence of antinode is a result of superposition of each term in the transfer function
