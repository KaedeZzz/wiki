- **Lumped mass model**: model can be made up of point masses (or rigid bodies) connected by massless springs.
- **Modal reduction**: the modes of a continuous structure can be identified, then the response can be approximated by only including a finite number of modes.
- **Galerkin approach**: the deformation of the structure is assumed to be a linear sum of suitable functions. The degrees of freedom are the magnitudes of each basis functions.
- **Finite element method**: the structure is divided into small sections, the deformation of each section is assumed to be given by a suitable function, and the pieces are stitched together to give the overall deformation.
