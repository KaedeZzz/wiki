#ESSENTIAL 
[[Equation of Free Vibration of String]]

EP1 Q4
Why would a fork produce tone that is *stronger* and *one octave higher* when its base is held fixed against some surface? It turns out that:
1. It produces tone of *an octave higher* because its predominant mode, antisymmetric mode (see EP for detail), produces secondary vertical oscillation whose frequency is twice the mode frequency (one left-right movement corresponds to two up-down or top-side movement of fork tips)
2. This vertical oscillation passes to the base, and when held fixed against a surface, leads the surface to oscillate as well.
