A set of intrinsic coordinates (*coordinate transformation*) may be determined by the ***left eigenvectors*** of the [[Koopman Operator]] on an invariant subspace. Explicitly:
$$
\psi_{\alpha}(\mathbf{x})=\mathbf{\xi}_{\alpha}\mathbf{y}(\mathbf{x}),\text{ where }\mathbf{\xi}_{\alpha}\mathbf{K}=\alpha \mathbf{\xi}_{\alpha}
$$
