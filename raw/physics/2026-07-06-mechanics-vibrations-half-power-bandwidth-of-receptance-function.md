The width of peak of receptance function is often characterised by the half power bandwidth, that is, the width of the function ad $-3dB$ of the peak.

Approximately:
$$
\Delta\omega=2\zeta_{n}\omega_{n}\text{ for }\zeta\ll 1
$$
on all three functions.
